---
name: Careers UX scope
description: Preserve recruitment criteria and terms when simplifying career pages and applications.
---

Treat easier-to-understand and easier-to-apply requests as UX improvements, not permission to change hiring policy. Preserve advertised eligibility, screening questions, compensation, hiring stages, and internship terms and benefits. Research and ML publication evidence must agree with the advertised role criteria; don't label that evidence optional merely because other paid roles share the questionnaire.

**Why:** The request concerned clarity and usability, not a lower hiring bar. Contradictory role requirements and form instructions mislead candidates.

**How to apply:** Simplify labels, guidance and presentation first. Keep frontend and server validation consistent through the shared questions. Ask before removing screening requirements or changing employment terms.
