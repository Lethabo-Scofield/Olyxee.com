New Orgni positioning:

Orgni is operational intelligence infrastructure that helps work move across a business.

Do not describe Orgni primarily as:
- a knowledge base
- enterprise search
- RAG
- a document assistant
- a chatbot
- a generic AI assistant
- a knowledge-management product

The business problem Orgni addresses:

Work inside companies constantly stops because people are waiting for:
- answers
- context
- documents
- approvals
- updates
- another employee
- information from another department
- routine work to be completed

Orgni reduces these blockers by understanding the organisation and helping people and systems get work done.

Core positioning:

“Keep your business moving.”

Supporting idea:

“Orgni understands your organisation, works across the tools your teams already use, and handles the questions and routine work that create unnecessary delays.”

Primary business outcomes:
- fewer blockers
- less repetitive work
- faster operations
- faster access to organisational context
- fewer unnecessary interruptions
- reduced dependency on specific people
- faster customer responses
- smoother coordination between teams
- routine work handled automatically where appropriate

Orgni must be presented as useful across the whole business, not just for interns, employees asking questions, or technical teams.

Examples can span:

- shipment status
- delays
- customer updates
- operational documents
- delivery coordination
- exception handling

Finance:
- invoices
- payment follow-ups
- reporting questions

- anomaly explanations

Operations:
- blockers
- ownership

- deadlines
- handoffs
- internal requests

Sales:
- client context

Customer service:

HR:

- scheduling

The experience should be understandable through examples such as:

“@Orgni where is shipment OLX-4821?”
→ Orgni checks the relevant business systems and returns the answer.

“@Orgni prepare everything for tomorrow’s customer meeting.”
→ Orgni gathers the relevant account, documents, conversations, open issues, and context.

“@Orgni what is blocking Project Atlas?”
→ Orgni identifies dependencies, owners, and unresolved work.

A customer emails asking for an invoice.
→ Orgni understands the request, retrieves the correct information, responds where authorised, and records what happened.
