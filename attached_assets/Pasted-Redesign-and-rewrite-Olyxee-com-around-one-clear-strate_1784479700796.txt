Redesign and rewrite Olyxee.com around one clear strategic position.

## Core objective

Olyxee must be presented as the company building Orgni.

Olyxee is not a general AI agency, document-intelligence company, automation consultancy, or collection of unrelated AI products.

Its long-term position is:

**Olyxee is an organisational intelligence company building the infrastructure through which intelligent systems understand and operate inside businesses.**

Orgni is Olyxee’s main product and company mission.

Order Loop must not appear on the Olyxee homepage. It will remain a separate commercial product on its own website.

## Main positioning

Use this hierarchy:

* Olyxee: the company
* Orgni: the core platform and mission
* Olyxee Research: foundational research supporting Orgni
* Careers: people helping build the mission

The homepage should make it clear that better AI models increase the need for Orgni rather than replacing it.

Models can provide general intelligence, but they do not automatically know:

* How a specific organisation operates
* What is currently happening inside it
* Which rules apply
* Who owns each responsibility
* What previous decisions were made
* What actions are permitted
* What outcomes followed earlier actions

Orgni provides this organisation-specific understanding.

## Homepage structure

### 1. Hero

Headline:

**Make your organisation understandable to intelligence.**

Supporting copy:

Olyxee is building Orgni, a living operational model and runtime that continuously understands how an organisation operates across its people, systems, processes, rules, decisions, events and outcomes.

Primary CTA:

**Explore Orgni**

Secondary CTA:

**Talk to Olyxee**

Do not use “Try Orgni” because the platform is still being developed.

### 2. The problem

Headline:

**Intelligence is becoming abundant. Organisational understanding is not.**

Explain that AI models can reason and act, but they do not automatically understand the unique operational reality of a company.

Business context is fragmented across:

* People
* Applications
* Databases
* Workflow events
* Decisions
* Approvals
* Communications
* Policies
* Documents
* Customer interactions
* Operational outcomes

Documents must appear as only one source, not the centre of Orgni.

Add a simple visual:

Models and agents
↓
Orgni
Organisational model
Operational memory
Permissions and rules
Execution context
↓
Business systems and operations

### 3. Introduce Orgni

Headline:

**Orgni is the living model of your organisation.**

Supporting copy:

Orgni continuously connects people, systems, processes, rules, decisions, events and outcomes into shared organisational understanding.

Present Orgni through three foundational layers.

#### Organisational Model

Represents:

* People
* Roles
* Teams
* Systems
* Processes
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Resources
* Rules
* Relationships

#### Operational Memory

Preserves:

* Events
* Decisions
* Changes
* Exceptions
* Outcomes
* Reasons
* Corrections
* Historical state

#### Intelligence Runtime

Provides people, software and intelligent systems with:

* Current context
* Permissions
* Applicable rules
* Available actions
* Required approvals
* Execution history
* Outcome feedback

Avoid making Orgni look like a chatbot, dashboard, workflow tool, RAG system, or document-processing product.

### 4. How Orgni learns

Headline:

**Business context is created through operations.**

Show the following sources:

* System events
* Database changes
* Workflow transitions
* Human decisions
* Employee actions
* Approvals
* Customer interactions
* Communications
* Policies
* Documents
* Exceptions
* Operational outcomes

Add this process:

**Observe → Represent → Understand → Support action → Measure outcome → Learn**

Supporting copy:

Orgni does not simply retrieve information. It maintains a changing model of what the organisation is, what is happening inside it and why.

### 5. Why better AI increases demand for Orgni

Headline:

**Built for a world of increasingly capable intelligence.**

Supporting copy:

As models become more capable, more organisations will allow intelligent systems to perform important work. Those systems will need accurate organisational context, permissions, operational state, memory and governance.

Orgni provides the organisation-specific infrastructure that models cannot bring with them.

Add a simple visual:

Better models
↓
More intelligent systems inside businesses
↓
Greater need for organisational context and control
↓
Greater value for Orgni

Use this supporting statement prominently:

**Models provide intelligence. Orgni provides organisational understanding.**

### 6. What Orgni enables

Headline:

**One organisational understanding. Many intelligent systems.**

Show capabilities such as:

* Persistent organisational memory
* Context-aware agents
* Workflow understanding
* Decision traceability
* Exception detection
* Impact analysis

* Cross-system coordination
* Continuous organisational learning

Clearly label capabilities that are still in development. Do not present future concepts as live production features.

### 7. Current development

Headline:

**Building Orgni from the foundations up.**

Show real current work:

* Organisational ontology
* Organisational tokenizer
* Normalised event and context contracts

Use status labels such as:

Do not use fake customer statistics, unverified performance claims, or fictional case studies.

### 8. Research

## Design direction

Use a distinctive, minimal and premium design.

The website should feel:

* Foundational
* Technical
* Clear
* Serious
* Future-facing
* Research-driven
* Enterprise-ready

Avoid:

* Generic AI gradients
* Excessive glowing effects
* Floating chatbot visuals
* Dense dashboards
* Stock illustrations
* Icon clutter
* Overloaded cards
* Long paragraphs
* Vague AI claims
* Generic SaaS landing-page layouts

Use:

* Strong typography
* Large amounts of whitespace
* Minimal navigation
* Structured diagrams
* Clear information hierarchy
* Subtle motion
* Short sections
* Precise language
* Technical visuals showing relationships and flows
* White or neutral background with restrained contrast

The site should resemble a serious infrastructure company rather than an early-stage AI tool.

## Technical requirements

* Build with Next.js and TypeScript.
* Ensure clean deployment to Vercel.
* Use responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile.
* Use reusable components.
* Use accessible semantic HTML.
* Optimise performance and image loading.
* Add proper metadata and Open Graph tags.
* Use clean URLs.
* Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
* Keep animations lightweight.
* Ensure the site has no broken links or placeholder pages.
* Remove old product routes or redirect them to the relevant Orgni or company pages.
* Add redirects for archived Ordo, Document Integrity and Enterprise Automation pages.

## Final standard

A first-time visitor should understand within ten seconds:

1. Olyxee is the company.
2. Orgni is its main product and mission.
3. Orgni helps intelligent systems understand how organisations operate.
4. Orgni becomes more valuable as models become more capable.
5. Olyxee is building foundational infrastructure, not another AI application.
