Products and solutions:

1. Ordo
Description:
Ordo is Olyxee’s business workflow execution system. It helps enterprises execute processes end-to-end by connecting AI reasoning with business systems, APIs, databases, approvals, and operational workflows.

Use cases:
- Workflow automation
- Business process execution
- Human-in-the-loop approvals
- API and system orchestration
- Operational decision execution

2. Addup
Description:
Addup is Olyxee’s financial reconciliation and data integrity engine. It helps finance teams compare, validate, and reconcile financial records such as bank statements, ledgers, and accounting data.

Use cases:
- Bank and ledger reconciliation
- Financial data validation
- Duplicate, mismatch, and anomaly detection
- Accounting integrity checks
- Audit support

3. Courier Loop
Description:
Courier Loop is Olyxee’s logistics communication and tracking infrastructure. It helps logistics companies provide delivery status updates, tracking links, and shipment communication through SMS, web pages, and system integrations.

Use cases:
- Delivery status updates
- Customer shipment communication
- Tracking pages
- Logistics notifications
- Order visibility

4. Olyxee Doc Integrity, also called ODI
Description:
ODI is Olyxee’s document integrity and validation solution. It helps organizations validate documents, extract structured information, detect inconsistencies, and support trusted decision-making from documents.

Use cases:
- Document validation
- Certificate checks
- Structured data extraction
- Inconsistency detection
- Compliance support

5. Togent
Description:
Togent is Olyxee’s AI integration and agent tooling layer. It supports the connection between AI systems, business tools, workflows, and enterprise environments.

Use cases:
- AI system integration
- Agent tooling
- Enterprise AI connectors
- Workflow intelligence
- Internal AI infrastructure

6. Olyxee Research
Description:
Olyxee Research is the research division behind Olyxee’s approach to reliable AI systems. It focuses on AI accuracy, document corruption, financial integrity, data validation, and trustworthy enterprise AI.

Use cases:
- AI reliability research
- Financial AI integrity
- Document corruption detection
- Benchmarking AI systems
- Research-backed product development
