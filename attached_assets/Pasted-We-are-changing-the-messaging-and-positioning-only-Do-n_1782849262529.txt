We are changing the **messaging and positioning only**. Do **not** redesign the UI, change branding, alter layouts, animations, colors, navigation, or overall visual style. This is a **content and information architecture update only**.

## Objective

Reposition Olyxee from an AI product company into a **research and infrastructure company**, with a single primary product: **Orgni**.

Our messaging should feel more foundational and long-term. We are no longer positioning ourselves as a collection of AI tools or modules.

---

## Company positioning

Update all website content so that Olyxee is consistently described as:

**Research and Infrastructure for Operational Intelligence**

The core thesis is:

> Olyxee builds the infrastructure that enables intelligent systems to understand and operate inside real businesses.

Avoid messaging that makes Olyxee sound like:

* an AI agency
* a chatbot company
* a workflow automation company
* a document AI company
* a collection of AI tools
* a generic enterprise AI platform

Instead, emphasize:

* operational intelligence
* live business context
* operational memory
* decision history
* infrastructure
* research
* model-neutral architecture
* intelligent operations

The tone should be serious, technical, and infrastructure-focused.

---

## Product strategy

We are simplifying the product strategy.

### Keep

* Orgni
* Order Loop

### Remove as separate products

Remove all references that position these as independent products:

* Orgni Finance
* Orgni Workflows
* Any other Orgni sub-products

These are no longer products.

If functionality still exists, present it as **capabilities inside Orgni**, not separate offerings.

For example:

Instead of:

* Orgni Finance
* Orgni Workflows

Use wording like:

* Financial operations
* Operational workflows
* Controls
* Business memory
* Context

These are capabilities of Orgni.

---

## Orgni positioning

Orgni is our flagship product.

It should never be described as:

* a chatbot
* a document assistant
* a document search tool
* a workflow builder
* an AI copilot

Instead, describe Orgni as a platform that builds **live business context** for modern operations.

Key idea:

Orgni continuously learns how a business operates by connecting knowledge, documents, decisions, processes, responsibilities, systems, permissions, and outcomes into a living operational context.

The emphasis is not on answering questions.

The emphasis is on making businesses understandable to intelligent systems.

---

Move toward:

## Olyxee website

The website should communicate:

Who we are.

Why operational intelligence matters.

Why live business context becomes more important as AI becomes more capable.

Why we are building infrastructure rather than another AI application.

The focus should be on our long-term vision, not feature lists.

## Orgni website

The Orgni website should explain:

* what live business context is
* why businesses lose operational knowledge
* how Orgni builds and maintains operational context
* why intelligent systems require this context

Do not overemphasize documents.

Documents are one source of operational context, not the product itself.

## Order Loop

Keep Order Loop on the website as a standalone product.

Do not change its visual presentation.

Do not describe it as part of Orgni.

Keep its positioning focused on helping businesses manage customer order communication and operational updates.

* redesign pages
