Redesign the public credential verification page completely.

The current page looks like an AI-generated certificate because it uses oversized centered text, excessive empty space, stacked certificate wording, decorative styling, and long content blocks. The physical certificate already provides the ceremonial design. This page should function as an official verification record.

Use a clean institutional layout:

- Standard Olyxee website header at the top.
- Light grey page background.
- Centered white record container with a subtle border and minimal shadow.
- Maximum width around 900px.
- No decorative certificate border.
- No script fonts, gradients, glass effects or oversized quotation marks.
- Use the same typography, colours and spacing as the main Olyxee website.

At the top of the record:

- Small green verified icon.
- Heading: “Credential verified”.
- Supporting text: “This credential was issued by Olyxee (Pty) Ltd.”
- Credential number displayed quietly on the right or underneath.
- Share button as a secondary action.

Main identity section:

- Small label: “Credential holder”
- Full name as the primary heading.
- Programme title directly underneath.
- Position displayed once only.
- Do not use wording such as “This certifies that” because this is a verification record, not the certificate itself.

Display dates in a compact information row:

| Internship period | Issue date | Status |
| 20 April – 20 July 2026 | 28 July 2026 | Verified |

Below that, use clearly separated sections:

- Areas of contribution
- Skills demonstrated
- Recommendation

Render projects and skills as clean bullet lists. Keep recommendation inside a simple pale-grey block with a thin left border. Show the supervisor’s full name and title below it.

Footer:

- Olyxee logo or wordmark.
- “Issued by Olyxee (Pty) Ltd.”
- “This is the authoritative online record for this credential.”
- Link back to olyxee.com.

Mobile requirements:

- One-column layout.
- Comfortable 20px side padding.
- Dates stack vertically.
- No text smaller than 14px.
- Keep the verified status visible immediately after opening.
- Avoid making the visitor scroll through large empty spaces.

The overall page must feel like a real corporate verification portal, not an AI-generated digital certificate. Think official university transcript verification or professional credential registry: restrained, structured and trustworthy.
